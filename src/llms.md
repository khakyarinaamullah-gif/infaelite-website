## How we work

- [Six-stage process]({{site}}#process): 1 Discovery and Strategy; 2 Research and Planning (competitors, keyword themes, sitemap, technical choices); 3 Creative Design (direction, then full page designs for mobile and desktop); 4 Development and Engineering (a Shopify theme, WordPress build or custom app); 5 Testing and Optimization (devices, browsers, forms, checkout, speed and SEO basics); 6 Launch and Support (go-live, redirects, monitoring, handover and support options).
- [Technology]({{site}}#stack): Shopify, Shopify Liquid, WooCommerce, WordPress, PHP, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, REST APIs and GraphQL.

What we build on Shopify.

Take one piece or hand us the whole store. Everything is written to Shopify’s theme standards, so apps, updates and your own edits keep working.

  • {{ic:store}}

    Custom Shopify themes

    A theme written for your brand in Liquid, with sections and blocks your team rearranges in the theme editor instead of sending a request for every change.

  • {{ic:pen-tool}}

    Figma to Shopify

    Approved designs turned into a working theme that matches them closely on desktop and phone. Mobile layouts are treated as designs in their own right, not shrunk desktops.

  • {{ic:layout-template}}

    Theme customization

    New sections, product page features and cart changes on Dawn, Horizon or a premium theme, kept in separate, documented files so they are easy to carry forward when the theme updates.

  • {{ic:database}}

    Metafields and templates

    Product, collection and page templates driven by metafields and metaobjects, so size guides, ingredients and specifications are edited in the admin, not in code.

  • {{ic:plug}}

    Apps and integrations

    Reviews, subscriptions, search, email and analytics apps set up and styled to match the theme, with custom code where no app does the job cleanly.

  • {{ic:arrow-right-left}}

    Migration to Shopify

    Products, customers and content moved from WooCommerce or another platform, with every old URL redirected so links and search rankings carry over.

How a Shopify build runs.

Six stages. Each one ends with something you can review before the next begins.

  1. 01

    Discovery & strategy

    A call about the business, the products and what the store has to achieve.

    You get a written brief with goals, scope and priorities.

  2. 02

    Research & planning

    Competitor stores, the sitemap, the app list and the theme approach are settled before design starts.

    You get a sitemap, an app plan and a timeline.

  3. 03

    Design

    A direction first, then full page designs for mobile and desktop, built from your brand.

    You get approved designs and a design system for the theme.

  4. 04

    Development

    The theme is built on an unpublished copy in your store, so nothing changes for shoppers while we work.

    You get a preview link to click through.

  5. 05

    Testing

    Templates, devices, browsers, cart and checkout, speed and SEO basics are checked and tuned.

    You get a QA report and speed measurements.

  6. 06

    Launch & support

    We publish the theme, check orders and tracking, then stay on for what comes next.

    You get a live store, a handover session and support options.

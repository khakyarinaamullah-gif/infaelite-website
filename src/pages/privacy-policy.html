The short version

  • There are no accounts, logins or newsletter sign-ups on this site.
  • We count visits with Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to see which pages and buttons are used. It records no names, email addresses or messages, and there are no advertising or social media trackers.
  • The contact form does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with a draft, and we only receive what you choose to send.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from this website itself. Two photographs on the home page load from Unsplash.

Project briefs and emails

The form in the contact section checks that the fields are filled in, then hands a pre-filled email draft to the email app on your device. Nothing is stored or transmitted by the website itself, and nothing reaches us until you press send in your email app.

When you email or call us, we receive your name, your contact details and whatever you tell us about your project. We use this only to reply, to discuss and quote for the work, and, if we work together, to deliver it. We do not sell it or add you to a mailing list.

We keep project correspondence for as long as it is needed for the enquiry or project and for our business records, and delete it on request where we are not required to keep it.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, how visitors arrive (for example from a search engine or another website), the type of device and browser, an approximate location at country or city level, and which buttons are used, such as opening a project or choosing a free consultation. When you use the contact form, it records which kind of request and which service you picked and that your email app was opened. It never records your name, email address, budget or message.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_* to tell one visit from another. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Advertising features are switched off. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the analytics cookies are not set, and Google receives only cookieless measurements.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

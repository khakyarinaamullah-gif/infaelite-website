The short version

  • There are no accounts, logins or newsletter sign-ups on this site.
  • The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The contact form does not send anything to a server. It opens your own email app with a draft, and we only receive what you choose to send.
  • Fonts, motion scripts and some photographs load from third-party services, which receive the technical details every web request carries.

Project briefs and emails

The form in the contact section checks that the fields are filled in, then hands a pre-filled email draft to the email app on your device. Nothing is stored or transmitted by the website itself, and nothing reaches us until you press send in your email app.

When you email or call us, we receive your name, your contact details and whatever you tell us about your project. We use this only to reply, to discuss and quote for the work, and, if we work together, to deliver it. We do not sell it or add you to a mailing list.

We keep project correspondence for as long as it is needed for the enquiry or project and for our business records, and delete it on request where we are not required to keep it.

Services that deliver this site

Your browser requests some files directly from these providers. Like any web server, they receive your IP address, the address of the file requested and your browser’s standard headers, and handle them under their own privacy policies.

GitHub Pages
Hosts this website and may log visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub Privacy Statement
Google Fonts
Delivers the Manrope typeface. Google states that the Fonts API does not set cookies. Google Fonts privacy FAQ
jsDelivr
Delivers the open-source animation libraries GSAP and Lenis. jsDelivr privacy policy
Unsplash
Delivers some of the photographs on the home page. Unsplash privacy policy

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use local storage to identify you and does not include analytics, advertising or social media tracking code. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it does.

Links to other websites

Links to LinkedIn, Fiverr and other sites open those sites, whose own privacy policies then apply. Email and phone links hand over to the apps on your device.

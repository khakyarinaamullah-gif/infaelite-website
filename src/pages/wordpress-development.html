How a WordPress project runs.

Six stages. Each one ends with something you can review before the next begins.

  1. 01

    Discovery & strategy

    We learn the business, the audience and the content the site has to carry.

    You get a written brief with goals, scope and priorities.

  2. 02

    Research & planning

    The sitemap, content model, plugins and hosting are chosen before design starts.

    You get a sitemap, a plugin list and a timeline.

  3. 03

    Design

    A direction first, then full page designs for mobile and desktop.

    You get approved designs and a plan for the block library.

  4. 04

    Development

    The theme and any custom features are built on a private staging site.

    You get a staging site to click through.

  5. 05

    Testing

    Devices, browsers, forms, checkout, speed, accessibility basics and SEO are checked and tuned.

    You get a QA report and speed measurements.

  6. 06

    Launch & support

    We move the site live, set up redirects and monitoring, and show your team how to run the editor.

    You get a live site, a handover session and support options.

WooCommerce or Shopify?

Both are good platforms for different jobs. We build on both, so we recommend whichever fits.

Choose WooCommerce when

  • {{ic:check}}You want full ownership of the hosting, the code and the data.
  • {{ic:check}}Content, articles and SEO publishing sit at the centre of the site.
  • {{ic:check}}The products or pricing need logic most platforms do not offer.
  • {{ic:check}}You already run WordPress and want the store in the same place.

Choose Shopify when

  • {{ic:check}}Selling is the main job and you want hosting, security and checkout handled for you.
  • {{ic:check}}Your team wants to run the store day to day without a developer.
  • {{ic:check}}You plan to lean on Shopify’s app ecosystem for features.
